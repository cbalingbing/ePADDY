ePADDY

Detect Now

Enhanced Pest Acoustic Detection Device for Rice (ePADDY) a state-of-the-art tool designed to aid farmers and end-users in detecting insect pests in stored rice through sophisticated sound analysis. By leveraging advanced acoustic detection technology, ePADDY offers a sustainable and efficient solution for monitoring and managing insect pests, ensuring better quality and protection of stored products.

INSECTS

S_Oryzae
The rice weevil (Sitophilus oryzae) is a stored product pest which attacks seeds of several crops, including wheat, rice, and maize.
R_Dominica
Commonly known as the lesser grain borer, a destructive, cosmopolitan beetle pest of stored cereal grains and products.
T_Castaneum
Commonly known as the red flour beetle, a major cosmopolitan pest of stored grains, flour, and processed foods.
